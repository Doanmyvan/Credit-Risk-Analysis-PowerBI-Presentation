# 💳 Credit Risk Analysis – Loan Default Insights (Power BI & PowerPoint)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project **analyzes loan default risk** among applicants using 35,000+ loan transaction records to **identifies high-risk borrowers profile**, helping financial institutions make data-driven credit decisions and **minimize potential losses**.

Power BI was used to calculate key metrics and generate interactive dashboards, while findings were presented in PowerPoint for executive storytelling.

## Company Background
The company is a financial technology (Fintech) platform that connects investors (lenders) with borrowers, enabling peer-to-peer lending outside the traditional banking system. The company facilitates loans where investors provide capital and borrowers repay with interest, generating 
profit for both investors and the company

## 🚀 Analytical Strategy
Break down borrowers by risk factors (income, debt-to-income ratio, credit history, past defaults) to identify patterns and high-risk segments. Use interactive dashboards to explore trends and insights.

## Problem Statement 
- With the rise in loan demand, assessing borrower risk and loan performance has become increasingly important.
- The dataset, spanning 2007–2014 and including the 2008 financial crisis, poses potential risk implications.
- Analyzing this data can uncover repayment behaviors, default trends, and key factors influencing credit risk

## 🎯 Objectives

- Assess the risk of loan default across applicants.
- Identify key factors driving credit risk.
- Provide actionable insights for lending decisions.
- Enable risk-based prioritization for loan approvals.

## 📊 Analysis & Key Insights

**Loan Classification:**
- Good loan:
 • Fully Paid
 • Charge off, default, late (recovery rate > 70%)
 • Current (Loan seasoning >40%)
- Bad loan:
 • Charge off, default, late (recovery rate <40%)
- Neutral: Else

**Top Risk Drivers**:
- Delinquent accounts
- Debt-related and Small business loans
- Low income / high Debt-to-income ratio
- Long/large loans
- High interest rates- Grades D–G / short credit histories

Trend Observations: Borrower defaults are concentrated in certain income brackets and loan types.

## ✅ Recommendations
Business Impact: Targeted monitoring and intervention could reduce potential default losses by 9%. 

- Implement risk-based loan approval criteria.
- Prioritize additional verification for high-risk segments.
- Monitor critical borrower metrics (debt ratio, credit history) regularly.
- Integrate dashboards into decision-making workflows for executives and credit officers.
